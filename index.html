Privacy & limitations

RAIL COMMAND has no login, analytics tracker, remote database or cloud sync. The published app code is public, but your records are not sent to GitHub. Browser data can be lost if you clear Safari website data, reset your device or remove browser storage. Keep an external backup. Offline support is enabled after the app has been loaded once, subject to browser caching.
